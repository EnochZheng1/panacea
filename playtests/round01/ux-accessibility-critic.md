# Panacea · round 1 · playtest by "UX / accessibility critic"

A UX and accessibility reviewer working from Nielsen's heuristics and Celia Hodent's game-UX framework. I measure contrast, type size, focus, keyboard use, feedback and error prevention, and I check whether the screen alone tells the player what happened.

**Verdict:** The printed-matter look is legible. Body text on paper measures 7–13:1 everywhere, and the overfill and disabled-control messages are some of the best error handling I have seen in a prototype. But the game cannot be played from the keyboard. The footer status bar contradicts the panel beside it. And the page that is meant to report the month's consequences prints "ALL QUIET IN THE WARD · Nothing to Report" in its largest type over a death notice and a coroner's verdict that names the player. The feedback loop, which should be the game's strength, is where clarity breaks down.

**Setup:**

- Build `0ccdab7` (frozen copy `build-r1`), port 47014, 1280×800 window.
- Seed `ux-1`: a fresh game played by hand from January to August 1885 (7 sends to press).
- Jump-ahead game: seed `ux-1 --play 5 --dose 1.5`, which starts in June 1885 after 5 scripted heavy-laudanum months. I played it by hand to September 1885 (3 more sends) to reach deaths, inquests and more letters.
- About 40 minutes of active testing.
- Tooling, all in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-panacea/5950a741-0a66-5ec4-aee9-4515d76a27d7/scratchpad/r1/ux-accessibility-critic/tools/`:
  - `contrast.py` measures the WCAG ratio in a screenshot region. The background is the median pixel; the text is the darkest or lightest 3% (the darkest 1% is also reported).
  - `act.py` clicks driver targets by their text.
  - `month.sh` sends to press, waits, and captures the edition top, middle and bottom.
- I used ffmpeg and numpy for a flash analysis of the pre-captured clip `audit/suite/20_press_moment.mp4`, because my own driver screenshots came about 1.5 s apart, which is too slow to measure flashing.

All screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-panacea/5950a741-0a66-5ec4-aee9-4515d76a27d7/scratchpad/r1/ux-accessibility-critic/`. Below, `D/` stands for that folder.

**Keyboard**

- Edition screen: 0 stops until something is clicked.
- Escape closes nothing. Enter activates the focused button (verified on STEEPING).

**Press-sequence motion (from the 30 fps reference clip)**

- A full-screen fade from dark to light: mean relative luminance 0.02 → 0.23 in about 5 frames.
- Then sheets sweep across at about **3 Hz for 4 cycles (≈1.6 s)**. Each transition changes 15–20% of the screen area by more than 10% relative luminance (`D/c_clip_pulse.png`, `D/c_press_sheet.png`).
- This sits at the WCAG 2.3.1 three-flashes-per-second threshold. There is no skip control and no reduce-motion option.

## What works

- **Body contrast is good throughout.** Ink on paper measures 7–13:1 even over the letterpress grain. The footer is 14–17:1 and tooltips are 12:1. Nothing I'd call body copy fails.
- **Overfill is a model of error prevention.** "IT OVERFLOWS" appears over the glass, the assay dims, ENTER IT IN THE BOOK disables, and the panel explains: "Not yet. Formula volume 8.5 oz exceeds bottle capacity 8 oz." Keep this pattern and apply it everywhere.
- **Disabled controls mostly say why:**
  - "0 stills free this month."
  - "Not yet: the Gazette wants savings and a testimonial first."
  - "Nothing to advertise. Enter a recipe in the formula book."
  - Ad-size tooltips give prices ("A full column: $11.00 a month").
- **Discoverability text is honest.** The shelf states "Click a jar to pour half an ounce. Right-click to take it back." Claim tooltips switch between "Click to set it in type." and "Click to strike it out."
- **Hover states on buttons and letter options are strong.** The full ink/paper inversion is unmissable.
- **The ledger cutting** ("Opened the month with $150.00 · +$4.80 Sales to regulars · −32c. Bottles… · −$5.00 Rent") is clear and trustworthy. It deserves a place above the fold.
- **Death at the customer's shoulder** is a strong visual-only warning that works without sound.
- **Scheele's green stays reserved for the product.** Paired with black rules, it never carries meaning on its own, so colour-blind players lose nothing.

## Disclosures

- I used a jump-ahead game (`ux-1 --play 5 --dose 1.5`) to reach deaths, inquests and more letters. It started in June 1885.
- I used `targets`, `read` and `state` to find click coordinates and quote text. I confirmed the footer/Works cash mismatch with `read`.
- I read `scripts/Main.cs` only to learn when the knell and till cues fire, since I can't hear them, and grepped for `FocusMode` to confirm buttons are meant to be focusable.
- The flash analysis used the pre-captured reference clip `audit/suite/20_press_moment.mp4`, because my own screenshots came too slowly. Its seed differs, but the press sequence is the same.
- Ctrl+A in the name field went through the driver's `key` action; that key combination may not have reached Godot as a real chord. The missing select-on-focus and the missing rename stand either way.
- The driver log showed only ALSA/audio warnings and no exceptions.
- When I switched games, the first `kill $(cat …pid)` hit the xvfb wrapper rather than the game process, so the old game kept running. I killed it by process ID before starting the new one. Only one game ran during play.

## Score

**6 / 10.** The page is beautifully legible and the bench's error handling is excellent. But there is no keyboard path, a stale status bar, and a headline that denies the deaths it sits above, and together these undercut the clarity of the core loop.
