# Panacea · round 2 · playtest by "Careful Newcomer"

A player new to business sims who reads every tooltip, slip, letter, cutting and notice, and checks that what the game says matches what then happens.

**Verdict:** This is a real improvement. The still works, the footer is live, the order form adds up to the cent, and the lead story finally reacts to deaths and inquests ("A SICKLY SEASON", "THE CORONER NAMES A REMEDY"). The new printed matter brings new contradictions, though. The footer's "this month" figure never agrees with the month in brief. The Coroner's Court names Mrs. Halloran as the dead woman on the same page where the lead names her infant. The paper says I "was not called to give evidence" after I testified at five inquests. With 7 letters waiting, SEND TO PRESS is pushed off the bottom of the screen. The books still add up; the words around them often don't.

**Setup:**

- Build: frozen copy at commit `f90405e`, port 47012.
- Game 1: seed `careful-3`, played by hand from the first edition (No. 600, January 3, 1885) to the September 1885 edition (No. 608). That is 8 sends to press.
- Game 2: seed `careful-4 --play 6 --dose 1.5`, a **jump-ahead** game. A scripted proprietor played Jan–Jun 1885. I took over at the July 1885 edition and played to the January 1886 edition (6 sends to press). I raised laudanum from 1.5 to 2 oz in October.
- Time: about 50 minutes of active testing.
- Tooling: the provided `drive.py`, plus small shell and Python filters (`rd.sh`, `ed.sh`) that dump `read`/`targets` text sorted by position, so I could quote it exactly. In game 2, a `month.sh` loop brewed 2×24 and pressed for four of the months. Screenshots and both driver logs are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-panacea/5950a741-0a66-5ec4-aee9-4515d76a27d7/scratchpad/r2/careful-newcomer/`.

## What works

- **The arithmetic is now trustworthy everywhere money is shown.** All 11 order totals and "ORDER ENOUGH" purchases matched the cash spent, the firing charge was exactly 3c a bottle every time, "Received with thanks: $6.00, paid on delivery." matched, and all 15 ledgers balanced.
- **The order form is lovely and clear.** "J. & W. HOBART & CO. · 81 Pearl Street", an order number, "Terms: cash on delivery", ON HAND / ORDERED / AMOUNT columns, and "Cash in hand $150.00" above the total.
- **The still now works and explains itself:** click a bottle to set the batch, "most the stock allows: 32", the fire and smoke on the engraving, and "On the fire: 24 bottles of …" for each still.
- **The footer is live.** It changed the instant I paid ($150.00 → $144.00) and after each letter.
- **The lead reacts to the worst news:** "A SICKLY SEASON / The Ward Mourns. / EIGHT DEATHS IN THE WARD THIS MONTH; UNDERTAKERS REPORT A BRISK TRADE." and "THE CORONER NAMES A REMEDY / A Verdict Against the Bottle." The markets follow too ("Coffins, pine, $5.20. Lilies, scarce.").
- **Letters say what they cost:** "Sent: “Send the fifty dollars.” It cost $50.00." The bench warns "☞ 1 letter unanswered: the usual reply will go out for you."
- **Naming fixes that do work:** DEATHS ("HALLORAN.—Infant child of Mrs. Bridget Halloran, aged 6 months"), the inquest letters, the proof's Coroner's Court slip, and "Mrs. Bridget Halloran will not be calling again. She is in mourning." "a Person Unknown" replaces the blank name.
- **The name box selects all on focus.** Typing replaced "Dr. ——'s Celebrated Panacea" at once.
- **Small honest touches:** "A regular for a month", "1 letter", "Reformulated. The next batch will be the new recipe; the customers will not be told.", the skeleton gentleman standing behind the gravely ill at the counter, and "Nobody has thought to look at the bottles."

## Disclosures

- **Jump-ahead:** game 2 used `careful-4 --play 6 --dose 1.5`. The first six months, the Halloran inquest and the "Doctor" title were the scripted proprietor's. For four of my six months there, a small script brewed 2×24 and pressed. I read every edition it produced.
- **State reads:** I used `state` to confirm cash after firing and to see unit cost and heat (heat 0.3 in February with claims and no ad). A player would not see those figures.
