# Panacea · round 2 · playtest by "Casual First-Timer"

A player who bought it on a whim from the Steam page because of the newspaper look. Plays management games now and then, reads only what's on screen, never opens a manual, and gives a game about 15 minutes to hook them.

**Verdict:** This time the paper talks back, and that hooked me. "THE HALLORAN INFANT", printed the month after I'd paid for a front page in which Mrs. Halloran praised my syrup, then "THE CORONER NAMES A REMEDY": that is the best thing in the game. I made and sold my first bottle in about four minutes with no help. The new letters, order form and still are a pleasure to use. Two things still stop me knowing whether I'm winning. The footer's "this month" figure disagrees with the paper's own "the books up/down" every month. And for eight months straight the lead was the same "BRISK TRADE ON MULBERRY STREET" with the same paragraph.

**Setup:**

- Build `f90405e` (frozen copy `build-r2`), port 47011, seed `casual-2`, fresh game, **no jump-ahead**.
- I played January 1885 to February 1886: 13 sends to press, about 40 minutes of active play. I then restarted the same seed once and replayed **only month 1** to re-capture screens I couldn't view the first time (see Disclosures).
- Tooling: `drive.py`, plus a tiny shell wrapper (`press.sh`) that clicks SEND TO PRESS, waits, screenshots the top, middle and bottom of the new edition and prints `state`. I also used one JSON click list (`buy24extra.json`) for filling in the order form a second time. Nothing I wrote makes decisions.
- Screenshots: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-panacea/5950a741-0a66-5ec4-aee9-4515d76a27d7/scratchpad/r2/casual-first-timer/` (the re-captured month 1 is in the `re/` subfolder).

Last round this mangled my name; this time it didn't (`re/r05-named.png`).
- **~3:00.** THE STILL. It says "The shelf is short of water: 3 oz a bottle, 0 oz on hand." next to a button reading **ORDER ENOUGH FOR 24 · $4.20**. One click filled 24 bottles on the shelf in green, the next lit the fire, and smoke rose from the drawing (`re/r07-still.png`, `re/r08-ordered.png`, `10-fired.png`). Round 1 took me 6 minutes and 15 clicks; this took 3 clicks.

## What works

- **The press now pays off.** "THE HALLORAN INFANT" with the same woodcut as the front-page testimonial a month earlier, then "THE CORONER NAMES A REMEDY / A Verdict Against the Bottle.", then "DEATH OF MR. WHITLOCK / Suddenly, in the Ward." The headline on the flying press sheet is the real one. Keep all of it.
- **THE MONTH IN BRIEF** is the right idea in the right place. "36 bottles sold · the books up $4.32 · 2 dead · 1 new customer · 1 gone." is the one line I read every month, and clicking it opens "The Month's Books", itemised, ending with "The silent partner keeps a second set of books. They are not shown." Just make the footer agree with it.
- **THE TRADE itemises the money.** "Takings $23.40; outgoings $15.48 (newspaper advertising $6.00, ingredient purchases $5.40, rent $3.00)." answers my round-1 complaint.
- **The still.** The engraved still, a shelf of 48 bottles that fill green as you click, smoke when it's lit, "The shelf is short of water: 3 oz a bottle, 0 oz on hand." and **ORDER ENOUGH FOR 24**. Making my first remedy needed no outside help.
- **The post as letters.** Letterheads (Office of the Coroner, Sanitary Bureau, The Eclectic Medical College of Ohio with a "FIFTY DOLLARS" seal), "In pencil, on the back of a handbill", "Nothing else in the post this morning.", the instant "Sent: … It cost $3.00." slip, and the bench warning "two inquests unanswered: the coroner will proceed without you."
- **The order form.** "J. & W. HOBART & CO. … Terms: cash on delivery." with a running TOTAL. It looks like a period document, not a spreadsheet.
- **The name box** selects all on focus and warns that the name is permanent. No more mangled names.
- **The humour lands harder because the paper reacts.** New favourites:
  - "Physicians observe that the remedy is purely vegetable, and that so is hemlock."
  - "The physician who attended declined to say more, except that the child had been very quiet."
  - "Mrs. Bridget Halloran, of the Bowery, is in mourning, and has no further need of the remedy."
  - "IN THE TRADE.—THE CORONER'S CONSULTING FEE. Having survived one inquest, you have learned that the coroner is a practical man."
  - "FOUND.—A brown bottle, three-quarters empty, in Baxter street. The owner will not be needing it."
  - "Weather: Cold rain. Pine coffins advance." and "Snow. Goodwill toward men, and toward the trade."
  - "THE ANTI-FAT CORDIAL … Twelve ladies of Brooklyn have vanished entirely."
- **More rival ads and wants rotate**: Anti-Fat Cordial, Dr. St. John's, Kline's Liver Pad, the night watchman, the clergyman. The side columns stayed fresh far longer than in round 1.

## Disclosures

- No jump-ahead, and no `--fake`, `--autopress`, `--pour` or `--clip`.
- **A screenshot-viewing limit hit me early.** The first ~20 screenshots of my main run (January to April 1885) came back unviewable when I opened them. For those months I played from `targets` and `read` dumps (button labels, tooltips and text), which a real player wouldn't have. So I **never saw the March and April 1885 headlines**. From May onward I viewed every screenshot. Afterwards I restarted seed `casual-2` once and replayed only January 1885 with the same choices, to see the first edition, proof, formula book, still, press and February edition with my own eyes (`re/r01`–`r12`). The narration of the first 4 minutes is from that replay.
- I used `state` after each press to log cash, sales and deaths. Every figure in the table was also on screen.
- I used `read` to quote some text exactly (THE TRADE lines, the Coroner's Court verdicts, the ad-unlock hint).
- In September 1885 my brewing clicks didn't register (`stillsFree` stayed 2). That was most likely my coordinates after the panel moved (finding 10), not a game bug. Finding 8 is about the missing warning, not the brewing.
- I can't hear, so nothing here is about audio.
