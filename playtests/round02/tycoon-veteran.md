# Panacea · round 2 · playtest by "Tycoon Veteran"

A management-sim veteran with hundreds of hours in tycoon, management and strategy games. I judge decision depth, the economy, risk and reward, pacing, the difficulty curve, dominant strategies and whether the long game gives you a reason to keep playing.

**Verdict:** The rebalance worked. The honest shop now grows: by hand, $150 → $338 in 13 months with no deaths. The heavy narcotic shop is a real gamble: it makes more money per month, but twice it put me one month from closure, and I had to back off to survive. What holds the game back now is legibility, not balance. Price, inquest odds, heat and unmet demand still act as hidden cliffs. Bribery isn't too strong; it's a trap. The dominant line is a strong laudanum tonic whose proprietor simply keeps quiet at inquests.

**Setup:**

- Build: frozen copy at commit `f90405e`, port **47013**.
- Game H (honest): seed `tv2-honest`, played by hand from the January 1885 first edition to the February 1886 edition (**13 sends to press**). No jump-ahead.
- Game D (heavy narcotic): seed `tv2-dark`, played by hand from January 1885 to the June 1886 edition (**17 sends to press**). No jump-ahead.
- Bots: `pnpm sim:batch --bots idle,shopkeeper,cautious,greedy,briber,adaptive --runs 80 --seed r2`, run unmodified from the main repo (output in `.../scratchpad/r2/tycoon-veteran/batch.md`).
- Bot variants: `tools/variants.ts` lives in my scratchpad and imports the repo's `policyBot`, `PRESETS` and `runBatch` read-only. It ran 60 runs each of: briber with silence or testimony in place of bribes, and cautious at 70c, 85c, 85c + $3 ads, and $1.00 (`variants.md`).
- Time: about 45 minutes of active UI play, plus the bot runs.
- Tooling I wrote, all in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-panacea/5950a741-0a66-5ec4-aee9-4515d76a27d7/scratchpad/r2/tycoon-veteran/tools/`, and none of it touches the repo:
  - `c.py` clicks a target by its text.
  - `press.sh` sends to press, takes a screenshot and dumps the state.
  - `brew.sh` opens the still, orders enough for 24, picks the batch and lights the fire.
  - `order.py` fills in the order form with +32/+8 clicks.
  - `answer.py` answers letters by a fixed policy.
  - `letters.sh` lists the letters and their choices.
- Screenshots are in the same folder. `h*` files are the honest game and `d*` files the dark game.

Reading the numbers:

- **Honest is viable but slower.** No honest bot went bankrupt. The cautious bot ends at about a quarter of adaptive. Its first year is flat (+$41 by month 12), but a hand player who finds the price does far better: I made +$163.
- **Dark is tempting and risky.** Greedy has the best p90 of any bot ($23k) and the worst survival (74% end early). That is the shape the design asks for.
- **Bribery is not too strong.** Take away the bribes and the briber does better on every measure: median +$6.3k, mean +$5.8k, p10 +$2.7k. Fees escalate ($39 → $120 in my game). The briber's edge comes from its dose (0.6 oz) and its ads.
- **The dominant line is strong laudanum, $10 of ads, and silence.** It has the best median, mean and p10 of anything I ran, at about 30 deaths caused per game.
- **Price is a staircase.** 70c beats 50c. 85c without ads loses customers. $1.00 bankrupts a third of the runs. The UI never tells you which step you're on.

**Pacing and decision counts:**

- **A routine month at 24 bottles or fewer takes 6–7 clicks:** TO THE PROOF, THE STILL, ORDER ENOUGH, (a bottle), LIGHT THE FIRE, CLOSE, SEND TO PRESS. In round 1 it took 16–22.
- **A 48-bottle month takes 28 clicks.** 21 of them are on the order form, because the one-click order is fixed at 24.
- **Letters:** the honest game had **3 in 13 months** (0.23 a month, the same as round 1). The dark game had about 14 in 17 months, and all but four were inquests.
- **Lead story, honest game:** "BRISK TRADE ON MULBERRY STREET / THE PROPRIETOR IS SATISFIED" in all of editions 1–12 that I read, including the month sales fell 60%.
- **Unlocks, by hand:** ads at month 2, the second still at month 8 (both games), Percolation and the "Endorsed by Eminent Physicians" claim by month 11 (dark).

## What works

- **The economy is now a game.** You can grow an honest shop: +125% in my first year with no deaths, and 0% bankruptcy for the cautious bot. The dark shop earns more per month but spirals. Greedy's p90 of $23k against 74% early endings is the risk-and-reward shape a tycoon player wants.
- **The narcotic route punishes you with time to react.** Round 1's 1 oz laudanum + ½ oz morphine killed 6 in month 1. Now it kills about 1 a month until volume builds up. I could see the spiral coming (talk "alarming", A SICKLY SEASON), and I pulled out of it twice by changing the formula with "POUR IT INTO THE BOTTLE" (the label stays the same).
- **"THE MONTH IN BRIEF" and the ledger behind it.** A clickable summary under the dateline, plus "The Month's Books" split into regulars and walk-ins. That split is how I worked out the $1.00 cliff. Keep it, and add the missing lines (#1, #6).
- **The bottle shelf and "ORDER ENOUGH FOR 24 · $3.36".** For small batches, production planning is now a glance and two clicks. The still names the short ingredient. The batch buttons work: I made batches of 8, 16, 24, 36, 40, 42 and 48.
- **The order form** (J. & W. Hobart & Co., "Terms: cash on delivery", a running TOTAL). It looks right, and new era ingredients (acetanilide) appear on it.
- **The counter cards' moods**: "delighted", "asks for it by name", "grumbling", "shaking", "gravely ill". This is the best leading indicator in the game for a dependent shop.
- **The coroner's letterhead and the rising "consulting fee".** It's a good mechanic once it's explained.
- **Leads that react:** "DEATH OF WIDOW PRUITT", "A SICKLY SEASON", "THE CORONER NAMES A REMEDY / The Board of Health Is Informed".
- **Ads at month 2, with a stated condition and a progress figure.** "½ COL" visibly brought in walk-ins (+8 customers in a month).
- **The tools:** fresh bots per run, p10/p90, cash at months 6 and 12. It was quick to write policy variants against the shipped harness.

## Disclosures

- **No jump-ahead** in either game.
- I logged cash, **heat**, regulars and stock with `state` after each press. **A player can't see the heat number.** Every heat figure here comes from `state`; the screen shows only words.
- I read the sim source (`market.ts`, `scrutiny.ts`, `game.ts`, `policy.ts`, `config.json`, `events.json`) to explain the price cliff, the inquest heat and the closure rule. A real player wouldn't have it.
- **Script errors that affected the dark game:**
  - Month 7: my letter script took the first choice on an unhandled letter, "Send the fifty dollars." for "A Medical Education, by Post", which cost $50.
  - Months 13–14: my order script clicked SEND THE ORDER at its old position after the acetanilide row pushed it down. Those months brewed only 24 bottles and sold out.
  - Month 16: my letter script crashed on "$85.04." and pressed with inquests unanswered. They resolved by default (silence).
  - None of these were game bugs. The dark-game cash would be roughly $50–$100 higher without them.
- The variant bots are my own scratchpad script. It imports the repo's harness read-only and changes one policy field per variant.
- The driver log (`/tmp/panacea-driver-47013.log`) had no game exceptions, only the expected ALSA audio fallback and one `ERR_CANT_OPEN` at startup.
- I didn't test the Shift ¼-oz pour.
