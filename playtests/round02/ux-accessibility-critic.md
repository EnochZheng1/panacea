# Panacea · round 2 · playtest by "UX / accessibility critic"

A UX and accessibility reviewer working from Nielsen's heuristics and Celia Hodent's game-UX framework. I measure contrast, type size, focus, keyboard use, feedback and error prevention, and I check whether the screen alone tells the player what happened.

**Verdict:** This round fixed most of what I asked for. The lead now reports the deaths. The month in brief puts the results above the fold. Escape works everywhere, and the press can no longer be clicked past. The new letterheads, seals and order form are the most legible printed matter in the game, measuring 9.6–13:1. You can now finish a whole month from the keyboard, but it is hard work. Focus is invisible on SEND TO PRESS and the footer. It is lost after every action inside a drawer, and the edition's cuttings can't be reached at all. Two new layout and feedback faults damage trust. SEND TO PRESS slides under the footer when the bench has letters and warnings. And the footer's "this month" figure disagrees with the month in brief on every edition.

**Setup:**

- Build `f90405e` (frozen copy `build-r2`), port 47014, 1280×800 window.
- Seed `ux-2`: a fresh game played by hand from January to August 1885 (7 sends to press). Months 1–2 were driven mostly by keyboard; months 3–7 by mouse, with keyboard spot checks.
- **Jump-ahead game:** `ux-2 --play 5 --dose 1.5`, which starts in June 1885 after 5 scripted heavy-laudanum months. I played it by hand to October 1885 (4 sends). The August month was done **by keyboard alone**, from the edition to the press.
- About 45 minutes of active testing.
- Tooling, all in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-panacea/5950a741-0a66-5ec4-aee9-4515d76a27d7/scratchpad/r2/ux-accessibility-critic/tools/`:
  - `contrast.py` (from round 1) gives the WCAG ratio for a screenshot region. The background is the median pixel; the text is the 3% extreme, with the darkest 1% also printed.
  - `act.py` clicks a target by its text.
  - `month.sh` sends to press and captures the edition.
  - `crop.py` zooms a region.
  - `tabwalk.py` presses a key N times, screenshots each press and diffs them.
  - `tabto.py` presses Tab until a focus ring shows on the top and bottom edges of a given rect, and counts the presses.
  - I recorded the press sequence with `ffmpeg -f x11grab` from the game's own Xvfb display: 20 fps at 320×200 for the flash analysis, and 10 fps at full resolution for the skip hint.

Below, `D/` is `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-panacea/5950a741-0a66-5ec4-aee9-4515d76a27d7/scratchpad/r2/ux-accessibility-critic/`.

**Mouse clicks per month:** 5 (THE STILL, ORDER ENOUGH, LIGHT THE FIRE, Escape, SEND TO PRESS). In round 1 it was 10 or more.

**Press-sequence motion** (my own x11grab capture at 20 fps; the game renders at about 10 fps in software):

- There is a full-screen fade from mean relative luminance 0.47 to 0.015 over about 0.25 s. It is a single transition.
- Then come 4 sheet sweeps, with luminance peaks at frames 48, 58, 69 and 80: a period of about 0.53 s, or **about 1.9 Hz** (it was about 3 Hz). Each sweep changes 14–32% of the screen by more than 0.1 relative luminance.
- Then the edition arrives as one dark-to-light change over 77% of the screen.
- **This is under the WCAG 2.3.1 limit of 3 flashes per second.** There is still no reduce-motion option.

**The still's fire flicker:** 2,100–3,200 pixels change between screenshots, about 0.3% of the screen, inside a 150×60 px firebox. It is far below the flash-area threshold. I find it comfortable.

**The letter fold:** a stamp lands, the letter folds away over about 1–2 s, and the letters below slide up by 280 px. Nothing flashes. The slide moves the next letter's reply slips under the pointer (see #12).

## What works

- **The lead story now tells the truth.** "THE CORONER NAMES A REMEDY / A Verdict Against the Bottle / The jury finds that a stranger 'died of poisoning by a proprietary medicine.'" is above the fold, in the biggest type, the month it happens. Round 1's worst clarity fault is gone.
- **The month in brief** ("35 bottles sold · the books up $4.35 · 2 dead · 1 inquest · 2 new customers.") is exactly the summary I asked for, at 11.7:1 under the dateline. One click opens the ledger.
- **The new printed matter is very legible.** Letterheads, sign-offs, slips and the order form measure 9.6–15:1, and the seals 11–13:1. The order form finally shows prices (PER OZ and AMOUNT columns, and "TOTAL $7.80").
- **Error prevention on the bench is now consistent:**
  - "2 letters unanswered: the usual reply will go out for you."
  - "two inquests unanswered: the coroner will proceed without you."
  - "SEND IT ANYWAY ▸" when nothing is for sale.
  - "The shelf is short of water: 3 oz a bottle, 0 oz on hand." with a priced "ORDER ENOUGH FOR 24 · $4.20".
  - "Not yet. Formula volume 8.5 oz exceeds bottle capacity 8 oz."
- **Escape** closes every cutting and drawer and returns focus to the lever. Hints now say so ("Click anywhere, or press Escape, to put it down.").
- **The press is safe from the mouse and comfortable to watch.** The footer is locked, a click lands on the edition, and the sweeps measured about 1.9 Hz.
- **The jars are keyboard-reachable.** Enter pours and Backspace takes back. The name field selects all on focus.
- **The 2 px double-rule focus ring** on paper buttons, jars, slips and the shelf is clear wherever it is drawn.
- **Hover on cuttings** (a heavy black margin rule plus a wash, with no tooltip covering the text) is a good, unobtrusive signal.
- **The assay bars** are now outlined in ink, so you can see where the scale ends.

## Disclosures

- I used a jump-ahead game (`ux-2 --play 5 --dose 1.5`), starting in June 1885, to reach deaths, inquests, more letters and ad space.
- I used `targets`, `read` and `state` to find coordinates, quote text and log cash. State told me that SEND TO PRESS had focus when no ring showed, which a player could not know.
- I read `scripts/Main.cs`, `scripts/UI/ProofView.cs` and `scripts/Driver.cs` only to confirm which control `FocusMain` focuses, where the skip hint is placed, and how the driver sends keys.
- **Shift+Enter (¼ oz pour) could not be tested.** The driver's `key` action sends a keycode with no modifier state, so the press arrived as plain Enter or was dropped. I make no claim about the Shift path or about Shift+Tab.
- The flash and hint analysis used my own x11grab recording of the game's Xvfb display. The game rendered at about 10 fps in software, so the timings are approximate.
- The drawer shade and the inactive right column: I did not re-test clicking the shade or clicking the footer under an open drawer this round.
- The driver log showed only the ALSA "ERR_CANT_OPEN" audio warning and no exceptions in either game.
