# Panacea · round 3 · playtest by "Careful Newcomer"

A player new to business sims who reads every tooltip, slip, letter, cutting and notice, and checks that what the game says matches what then happens.

**Verdict:** Most of my round-2 complaints are fixed, and the fixes hold up. The footer and the month in brief agreed in all 19 editions. SEND TO PRESS stays in view with 6 letters waiting. Letter dates stay put, the coroner keeps one name per game, and the paper now reflects my testimony. Two new problems hurt trust. When every still is lit, the still drawer collapses into a column one letter wide. The inquest risk lines say testifying or silence costs you "if the jury names your bottle", but the suspicion gauge jumps by 20 or 28 the moment you click. Around them, the generated text still contradicts itself in small ways: "for her infant's a teething infant", a 3-month-old infant who was "a regular for 5 months", "Peacefully" under a lead that says "Suddenly", and "TRADE DULL" in a month that sold out.

- Tooling: the provided `drive.py`, plus small shell helpers in my scratch folder. `rd.sh` and `ed.sh` dump the `read`/`targets` text sorted by position, so I could quote it exactly. `brew.sh` opens the still, clicks bottle N on the shelf, presses ORDER ENOUGH and lights the fire. `press.sh` and `month.sh` press and dump the edition. ## What works

- **One figure, one definition.** The footer, the month in brief and the ledger agreed in 18 of 19 editions, and itemised outgoings now add up every time ("outgoings $69.08 (events $50.00, ingredient purchases $9.00, newspaper advertising $6.00, rent $3.00, bottles, corks and labels $1.08)"). Small amounts print without a double stop ("the books up 64c.;").
- **SEND TO PRESS stays put.** With 6 inquests, "And 4 more: see your letters." and two ☞ warnings, the lever stayed at y=652–722 and the type case scrolled instead (`241-j-nov-proof.png`).
- **Letters are better.** Each has its own voice and docket line ("Served at the shop by a boy from Centre street.", "In pencil, on the back of a handbill", "Doc, the tonic dont do what it did."). Engraved seals: an hourglass for the coroner, a diploma for Cincinnati. Dates stayed fixed, and every one fell before its proof.
- **The paper now hears my replies.** "Whatever its proprietor had to say for the purity of his preparations did not detain the jury long." The notice says "Having survived one inquest" (the right count). A bought verdict reads "Your envelope reached the coroner. The verdict is the one you paid for." The fee rises as promised ($35 → $50 → $60 → …), and the pay button disappears when you can't afford it.
- **Infants are named consistently** in DEATHS, the court reports and the lead ("THE SULLIVAN INFANT / THE INFANT CHILD OF MRS. SULLIVAN"; "An inquest upon the body of the infant child of Mrs. Theresa Abernathy").
- **The still shelf, when idle, is excellent.** You can mark beyond the stock: "You have marked 40; the stock on the shelf makes 32. Order the rest, or light the fire for 32." ORDER ENOUGH charges only the shortfall. The arrow keys work after a click (14 ↔ 16).
- **Selling out is visible:** "The shelf was bare before the month was out, and callers went away empty-handed."
- **The whole ad opens its cutting**, and the "Doctor" notice names the claim it adds ("the type case now holds a new claim: Endorsed by Eminent Physicians").
- **Lovely small touches:** "VOL. XVI" in 1886, "Lilies, scarce" and coffins at $5.05 in the worst month, the ledger's "Suspicion in the ward rose 46 · Inquest verdicts", and "Physicians observe that the remedy is purely vegetable, and that so is hemlock."
