## Still open

**Major**

- **The heavy route's survivors win, but most die young.** That is now the designed shape. Watch
  whether a 69% early ending reads as fair once players see it.
- **The wholesale contract pays back in about a year.** The design asked for payback over years.
  A lower price cap would slow it.

**Minor**

- The honest bitters line is 1.37× adaptive. The gap comes from 3 oz of cheap spirit.
- The counter cuts vary heads, not bodies. The three mothers hold the same baby.
- The still's cutaway and the flames are still simpler than the cut.
- Leader dots on the price current.
- "SOUND: ON" is a modern toggle.
- The opening cast's first names are not yet paired with surnames by origin.
- The Feeney reply still says "reputation" where the game says "standing".
- Godot has no export presets yet, and the hyphenation dictionary will need an include filter
  (`*.dic`).

## Lessons for round 5

- **A pipeline step that isn't checked will be skipped.** The re-cut seals sat behind stale crops
  for a whole round. Now a check fails the build.
- **"Every number checks out" moves the bar to words.** Once the books are trusted, the careful
  reader reads the copy as evidence. Each generated sentence must come from the same record as
  the numbers beside it.
- **The long game needs things to buy, not just things to survive.** The ventures turned a
  levelling-off honest shop into one that grows for ten years. Test them by hand next round.
