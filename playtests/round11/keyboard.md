# Panacea · round 11 · playtest by "The Keyboard-Only Player"

I have a repetitive strain injury and can't use a mouse. I play everything with Tab, the arrow keys, Enter, Space and Escape. I had never seen this game before.

**Verdict:** I could play Panacea from the title page to its twelfth edition without touching the mouse, and that is rare. Every drawer, letter, still, formula, supply order, venture and menu works by keyboard. Focus mostly lands on the next sensible thing, and a normal month takes about ten key presses. What I couldn't get at were the long scrolling lists (ALL THE REGULARS, the BOOKS history, the overflow in NOTES FOR THE PROPRIETOR) and everything the game explains only in hover tooltips. That includes the formula book's own keyboard instructions and the explanation of suspicion and death risk.

**Setup:** build `64dc5d8` (the frozen copy at `scratchpad/build-r11`), port 47135, seed 1111, `--player --profile keyboard`. I did not jump ahead. I played about 45 minutes of active time: 11 sends to press, January to December 1885. I wrote one tool, `k.py`, in my scratch folder. It sends a list of `key` actions, then `{"focus":true}`, and can take a screenshot. I sent no `click`, `move` or `scroll` actions at all. Screenshots are in `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-panacea/5950a741-0a66-5ec4-aee9-4515d76a27d7/scratchpad/r11/keyboard/`. All the NN_*.png names below are in that folder.

## What works

- **Focus goes where I want it.** The paper opens on TO THE PROOF. The proof opens on whatever the month needs: FORMULA in a new shop, THE STILL when the shelf is empty, SEND TO PRESS when there's stock. Closing a drawer returns focus to its tab. After ORDER, focus moves to LIGHT THE FIRE. After a reply, the next letter opens with focus on its first reply.
- **Escape always means "put it down".** It closes drawers, clipping cards and the settings screen, and on the paper or proof it opens the menu. The menu (BACK TO THE SHOP / SETTINGS / SAVE, AND THE TITLE PAGE) is fully keyboard-driven.
- **The still's bottle shelf** has ▲▼ for a dozen and ◀▶ for one, printed on screen. Keep that as the model for the formula book.
- **The counter is one Tab stop, walked with ◀▶.** That keeps the Tab order short, and the reversed card is easy to see. Down leaves it.
- **Two-press confirm on ventures** ("PAY $40.00 DOWN? PRESS AGAIN"). Keep it.
- **BOTH STILLS · ORDER AND LIGHT** brews on every still with one key press.
- **The VENTURES prospectus scrolls to follow focus**, panel by panel. The paper scrolls with PageDown and with Tab. The type case scrolls to follow focus on its lower claims.
- **Typing the remedy name** works: Tab to the field and type. The field selects all of its text on focus.
- **Save, quit and CONTINUE are exact,** both through the menu and after a hard stop mid-month.

## Disclosures

- No jump-ahead. One game, seed 1111, played with `--player --profile keyboard`.
- I used `{"focus":true}` after every key press. A real player would rely on the screen alone, and my claims about visible focus come from the screenshots.
- After the first session (months 1–11) I used `targets` and `read` to get the tooltip text and the hidden overflow text. I read `UI/ProofView.cs` (lines 680–760, 824, 1150–1330) and grepped `UI/FormulaBench.cs` for tooltips, to confirm findings 2, 3 and 6. I didn't change anything.
- The driver didn't deliver `Shift+Tab` or other key combinations with modifiers, so I couldn't test reverse-Tab or Shift-pour.
- I can't hear, so I make no claims about sound.
- The driver log had no exceptions, only ALSA "no sound card" lines at start-up.

## Score

**7 / 10.** For a keyboard-only player this is one of the best-behaved games I've tried: every action is reachable and focus nearly always lands where it should. What holds it back is that the lists and explanations a careful proprietor needs are behind scrolling or hovering, which I can't do. Making the scroll panels focusable and putting focused tooltips in a fixed strip (findings 1 and 2) would move it to 8.
